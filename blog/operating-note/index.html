Read the amendment before trusting the renewal date

Leasewell editorial · 14 November 2026 · 4 min read

AI reads commercial leases and amendments, then lists the obligations that need action.

Start with the input the team already has.

A property portfolio contains long agreements, repeated amendments and changing obligations. The AI must find the relevant clause and check whether a later document changes its meaning.

For commercial property and asset managers. Leasewell turns long lease packs into a dated action list, with the rent, renewal and notice clause beside each recommendation.

Make the review practical.

  1. Treat the lease and its amendments as one review package.
  2. Distinguish an extracted date from a legally confirmed notice deadline.
  3. Show the calculation behind rent and area-based obligations.
  4. Keep the reviewed document version with every assigned action.

One concrete example.

The original lease says 90 days’ notice. A later amendment changes that period to 120 days.

The prepared brief identifies the amendment and attaches both passages to the renewal action.

Ask the property manager and appointed adviser to confirm the applicable notice date.

Keep the decision with the responsible person.

A property manager checks the source and records the follow-up. It supports lease administration. Legal interpretation, contractual notices and commercial decisions require the appointed professional.

The product’s sample workspace lets you follow that chain: source, finding, review and retained action. The example is prepared for demonstration and does not claim measured customer performance.
